About TechLink Rwanda

Connecting customers with trusted technicians.

Reallife Problem

Many people in Rwanda face difficulties when they need a technician at home or at work. People often depend on WhatsApp groups, friends and relatives to find someone who can solve their problem.

This can make it difficult to identify a technician who is skilled, experienced and trustworthy.

Platform Solution

TechLink Rwanda provides an organized online platform where technicians can register their information and customers can search for technicians based on their service and location.

Customers can view technician profiles, experience, verification status and ratings before contacting them.
