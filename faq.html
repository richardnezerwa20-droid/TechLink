Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers about TechLink Rwanda.

What is TechLink Rwanda?

TechLink Rwanda connects customers with skilled and verified technicians for services such as electrical work, plumbing, computer repair and painting.

How do I find a technician?

Open the Find Technician page, choose the service you need and browse the available technicians and their details.

Are the technicians verified?

We aim to connect customers with reliable professionals. Review each technician's profile and contact them to discuss your requirements before booking.

How can I become a technician on TechLink?

Select Register in the navigation and complete the registration form with your service information and contact details.

Which areas do you serve?

TechLink Rwanda helps customers find technicians across Rwanda. Availability depends on the technician and the service location.

How can I contact TechLink Rwanda?

You can reach us at +250 788 000 000 or by email at info@techlink.rw.
